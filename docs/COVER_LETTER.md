## PROPOSAL ABSTRACT

This presentation explores how contemporary legal curricula must integrate Do-It-Yourself (DIY) legal workflows, ReNo cost calculation (Rechtsanwalts- und Notarfachangestellte fee structures), and legal software licensing into administrative and pedagogical frameworks. Key topics covered include:

- ReNo Cost Calculation upon DIY: Recalibrating legal support billing, administrative overhead, and fee structures when modular legal tools bypass traditional intermediaries.
- License Economics & Barcode Bank Integration: Examining standardized font metrics (MAIN.FNT), document indexing, and physical-to-digital asset management within legal frameworks.
- FAT Obligations & Legacy Persistence: Maintaining strict compliance, audit trail integrity, and file system governance across legacy DOS and modern legal software environments.

By connecting administrative fee economics with computer science patterns, this session equips legal educators with actionable paradigms to foster technological literacy and economic transparency among future practitioners.
